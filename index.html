    II
    01

    Jupiter Leo 25°28' · North Node Aquarius 25°30' · orb 0.03°, exact 2:00 PM · Mars Leo 23°47', 1.7° behind Jupiter

    Jupiter opposite the North Node, with Mars on board

    The Nodes are not planets. They mark the Moon's path, and they read as the collective's direction. Across the Leo and Aquarius axis, the South Node in Leo is where we have been: the single heart on the stage, the hero who does it alone. The North Node in Aquarius is where the group is going: the circle, the network, the we. Today Jupiter, the planet of faith and enlargement, sits exactly on that line, and Mars catches Jupiter by Mon Nov 16.

    Why it feels like too much

    Everything Leo gets bigger. The urge to be the one who holds it, saves it, carries it. Pride that will not ask. Generosity that is secretly a way to stay in control. Mars adds heat: the itch to act alone, now, and be seen doing it. Or the reverse: feeling invisible in a group, unsure there is room for your fire.

    What it is building

    A heart that shines for the circle, not instead of it. Jupiter magnifies whatever it touches, and on the South Node it magnifies the old way until it is plainly too heavy to carry alone. The North Node is the way out: offer the gift to the group and let the group take some of the weight. Both October portals were built on the receiving thread. This morning it is written across the whole sky.

    What is worth keeping

    VI
    1

    A symptom is an invitation, not a defect

    Ask the body what it needs before telling it what to do. Feel the symptom rather than decode it. The voice in your head that mocks the practice is the sign you are in the right spot.

    Don't you feel stupid doing this? Oh good, now I'm in the right spot.Sep 23

    The practice

    Body scan crown to root. Long exhales. Feet in the dirt. Ask the body, then wait for the answer.

    2

    Receiving is half of reciprocity

    Letting someone help you is a gift to them. Nothing needs to be earned today. The apology for taking up space becomes thanks for being held.

    We're going to start changing our words from I'm sorry to thank you.May 27 · verbatim

    The practice

    Left hand on the sacral, right on the solar: "Nothing is being taken from me. Nothing needs to be earned today."

    3

    Trauma contracts. Intuition is still.

    The fear voice speaks in "always" and "never" and wants it decided now. The true voice is calm, and usually says one thing. Bet on yourself and follow the breadcrumb, not the whole map.

    A vision is more powerful than a plan.Jul 21 · from the Wave summary

    The practice

    The body yes and no test. Then: which voice do I trust right now, the one that wants to act, or the one that wants to rest?

    4

    Endings are plantings

    Grieve the old form of the relationship, not necessarily the person. Death and birth run together. And when someone you love is in it, what they need from you is not the right words.

    You have not been buried, you have been planted.Sep 23

    The practice

    Stop plugging the cracks and let the water through the dam. Then show up: what is required of you is not your perfection, but your presence.
